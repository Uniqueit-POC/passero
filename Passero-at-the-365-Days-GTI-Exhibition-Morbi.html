The Grand Unveiling: Passero at the 365 Days GTI Exhibition, Morbi

In a significant stride toward expanding its footprint across the Indian market, Passero Vitrified LLP proudly participated in the prestigious 365 Days GTI Exhibition held in Morbi, Gujarat, throughout 2024. This landmark participation marked a pivotal moment in the brand’s journey, reinforcing its commitment to innovation, quality, and design excellence.

The exhibition served as a powerful platform for Passero Vitrified to showcase its advanced tile collections and evolving product portfolio, highlighting solutions crafted to illuminate Indian homes and commercial spaces with enduring brilliance and long-lasting performance. Through strong engagement with architects, dealers, and industry professionals, Passero further established itself as a forward-thinking brand shaping the future of architectural surfaces.
