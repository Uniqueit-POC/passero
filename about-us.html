A Legacy of Design, Innovation & Craftsmanship

Passero Vitrified is a premium vitrified tile manufacturer known for its unique designs, wide surface variety, and reliable quality. Every collection is inspired by nature and crafted with precision to enhance homes, commercial spaces, and large architectural projects.

With a direct-to-customer approach, Passero is growing its network of exclusive brand stores to give customers and designers a more personal, transparent, and hassle-free tile selection experience.

At Passero, every tile is a blend of art, engineering, and enduring elegance.

Our Commitment & Direction

At Passero Vitrified, our mission is to create vitrified tiles that bring together beautiful design, strong durability, and constant innovation. Every tile we make is crafted to enhance the space it belongs to, helping homeowners, architects, and designers turn their ideas into reality.

Our vision is to become one of India’s most trusted and design-focused tile brands by growing our network of exclusive brand stores and building a strong presence across the country and beyond. Through advanced manufacturing, modern technology, and strict quality standards, we strive to deliver a better customer experience at every stage.

Innovation

We regularly introduce new designs and surface finishes inspired by nature to keep our collections fresh and trend-setting.

Quality First

Every Passero tile is produced with strict quality checks to ensure strength, consistency, and long-lasting performance.

Customer-Centricity

From our brand stores to after-sales support, we are committed to making every customer experience smooth and reliable.

Sustainability

Our manufacturing process is designed to reduce waste and follow eco-friendly practices for a better future.

State-of-the-Art Manufacturing Facility

Our world-class manufacturing facility is equipped with modern machinery, automated production lines, and strict quality control processes. Every tile is crafted with precision using advanced technologies to deliver exceptional strength, consistency, and finish.
