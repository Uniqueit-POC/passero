A Legacy of Design, Innovation & Craftsmanship

Passero Vitrified is a leading manufacturer of premium vitrified tiles known for their design excellence, technological precision, and global quality standards. With a focus on innovation and craftsmanship, we create surfaces that elevate homes, commercial spaces, and large-scale architectural projects.

Built on a direct-to-customer vision, Passero is expanding its own exclusive brand stores, offering customers and designers a more immersive, transparent, and personalized tile selection experience.

At Passero, every tile is a blend of art, engineering, and enduring elegance.

Our Commitment & Direction

At Passero Vitrified, our mission is to craft exceptional vitrified surfaces that combine design, durability, and innovation. Every tile is engineered to enrich the spaces it becomes part of, empowering homeowners, architects, and interior designers with materials that inspire creativity and deliver lasting performance.

Our vision is to grow into India's most trusted and design-driven tile brand, expanding through exclusive brand stores and building a strong global presence. We aim to redefine the customer experience with world-class manufacturing, advanced technology, and a commitment to quality at every step.

Innovation

We continuously develop new designs, textures, and technologies that set trends in the tile industry.

Quality First

Every tile is manufactured with precision, strict quality control, and long-term durability in mind.

Customer-Centricity

Sustainability

State-of-the-Art Manufacturing Facility

Our world-class manufacturing facility is equipped with modern machinery, automated production lines, and strict quality control processes. Every tile is crafted with precision using advanced technologies to deliver exceptional strength, consistency, and finish.
