Standardized Packing & Handling

Passero Vitrified follows standardized packing procedures to ensure tiles are protected during handling, storage, and transportation. Each package is designed to maintain product quality, minimize breakage, and support efficient logistics for both domestic and international shipments.

Export-Ready Packaging & Safety Measures

Passero Vitrified follows export-ready packaging standards to ensure tiles remain safe and intact during long-distance transportation. Our packing methods are designed to withstand handling, stacking, and transit challenges across domestic and international supply chains.

Reinforced Cartons

Heavy-duty cartons provide added strength and protection during transit and stacking.

Protective Layering

Tiles are separated with protective sheets to prevent surface scratches and edge damage.

Secure Palletization

Every role contributes to maintaining our high standards of excellence.

Clear Export Labeling

Each package includes clear labeling for handling instructions, product details, and shipment identification.
